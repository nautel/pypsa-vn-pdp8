# Citation

If you use PyPSA-VN-PDP8, its data or its results, please cite the archived release:

> T. Le, H. C. Vu, M. Q. Duong, *PyPSA-VN-PDP8: an open capacity-expansion model of Vietnam's power system (data,
> results and code)*, Zenodo (2026), doi:[10.5281/zenodo.24037833](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24037833).

```bibtex
@misc{pypsa_vn_pdp8,
  author    = {Le, Tuan and Vu, Hai Canh and Duong, Minh Quan},
  title     = {{PyPSA-VN-PDP8}: an open capacity-expansion model of {Vietnam's} power system (data, results and code)},
  year      = {2026},
  publisher = {Zenodo},
  doi       = {10.5281/zenodo.24037833},
  url       = {https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24037833}
}
```

The DOI refers to all versions of the archive and resolves to the latest one; each version also has its own DOI on
Zenodo. The citation metadata are also in
[`CITATION.cff`](https://github.com/nautel/pypsa-vn-pdp8/blob/main/CITATION.cff), which GitHub shows under "Cite
this repository". A paper describing the model and its results is in preparation.

PyPSA-VN-PDP8 is built on [PyPSA](https://pypsa.org) (Brown et al., 2018, *Journal of Open Research Software*
6(1), 4) and [atlite](https://github.com/PyPSA/atlite), and follows the workflow designs of
[PyPSA-Eur](https://github.com/PyPSA/pypsa-eur), [PyPSA-Earth](https://github.com/pypsa-meets-earth/pypsa-earth) and
[PyPSA-USA](https://github.com/PyPSA/pypsa-usa).
